Acoustic Binary Image Transmission with Adaptive RLE Compression and Hamming Error Correction: System Design and Performance Analysis

IEEE Xplore · 22 september 2026
  • Modelled an end-to-end acoustic image link over an AWGN channel with a three-tap multipath component, comparing non-coherent FSK against coherent BPSK from −5 dB to 20 dB SNR.
  • BPSK reached BER 10⁻⁴ at roughly 8 dB SNR, about 3 dB better than FSK, while FSK held the smaller multipath penalty — 1.5 dB against 3.0 dB.
  • Hamming (7,4) ECC cut post-decoding BER about 26× at 12 dB SNR; the lossless-recovery threshold stayed at 12 dB from 16×16 to 32×32 pixels.
  • Adaptive RLE reached compression ratio 7.11 at 32×32, cutting transmission time from 8960 ms to 1260 ms.
  • Hardware validation on ESP32 (built-in DAC) and Raspberry Pi Pico (PWM audio) is the planned next step.
